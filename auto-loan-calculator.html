Auto Loan Calculator 2026: Get the Best Rate & Avoid Dealer Traps

Average new vehicle prices crossed $48,500 in early 2026 and average APR for new-car loans sits at 7.10% for prime borrowers. Knowing your true cost — including trade-in, tax, and dealer fees — is your strongest negotiating tool.

Get Pre-Approved Before You Walk In

Pre-approval from your bank or credit union eliminates dealer financing markup (the "dealer reserve"). Always negotiate car price separately from financing.

New vs. Used: Which Loan Is Better in 2026?

New car loans typically carry lower APRs than used car loans — often 1–2% lower. However, new vehicles depreciate 15–25% in their first year. A certified pre-owned (CPO) vehicle that is 2–3 years old offers a middle ground: lower price, lower depreciation risk, and manufacturer-backed warranty. Use our auto loan calculator to compare the true cost of new vs. used before you decide.

How Your Credit Score Affects Your Auto Rate

  • Super Prime (781–850): ~4.5–5.5% APR
  • Prime (661–780): ~6.5–7.5% APR
  • Near Prime (601–660): ~9–11% APR
  • Subprime (501–600): ~13–17% APR
  • Deep Subprime (below 500): ~18–25%+ APR

Improving your credit score before applying for an auto loan can save you thousands. Even moving from Near Prime to Prime can cut your monthly payment by $50–$100 on a typical loan.
